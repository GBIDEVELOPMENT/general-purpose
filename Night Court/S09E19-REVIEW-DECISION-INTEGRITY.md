# S09E19 editorial decisions: constrained original untimed evidence

The [11-task editorial queue](seasons/S09/S09E19/derived/editorial-review-v1/untimed-scene-review-queue.json) is now paired with `tools/validate_s09e19_review_decisions.py` and an immutable all-PENDING [reviewer decisions template](seasons/S09/S09E19/derived/editorial-review-v1/review-decisions-template.json). The five possible original dialogue paragraph matches remain **source-untimed**; each machine-proposed AVI interval is a separate hypothesis. Six sampled-image source observations remain unaccepted until original **continuous-video** review.

The validator requires the original video SHA and original 347-paragraph source SHA for every citation, complete coverage of the **candidate AVI interval** by each evidence method used on an accepted claim, an explicit source reviewer identifier and note, and a declared audit method. A purported dialogue acceptance must attest to *listening to original audio* and *auditing the original untimed paragraph*. Naming any speaker additionally requires original continuous video and mouth/voice corroboration. Asserted vocal delivery additionally requires isolated original-voice listening, since soundtrack pitch and subtitle text cannot prove anger, sarcasm or inflection. Stage action acceptance requires separately watched original continuous video. No source paragraph timestamp may be inserted into decision records, and no original paragraph text or source files are modified.

**Epistemic limit:** a schema-valid review can still be falsely attested. This utility validates claimed method, provenance, source time range and internal consistency. It neither plays original media, authenticates the reviewer nor independently verifies the attestation. Future production scripts must undergo separate QA acceptance; original 347 paragraphs stay untimed. Current accepted counts are **0/11 S09E19 review tasks and 0/193 complete production scripts**, with a strictly read-only synthetic-test CI runner that never uses FTP credentials.
