## What the partial source-frame comparison found

Before the original Qwen run, [original source-SHA-linked frame sampling](research/qwen-physical-business-preregistered-original-candidate-windows-2026-10-25.json) fixed the tested windows. After inference, the existing private frame archives were reviewed again at the **same ten nominal 5fps times per model clip**. Both are **source-derived still sequences**, not the actual original movie played with synchronized audio or independently verified native frame PTS.

In S01E01's 846–848s ten-frame sequence, the **seated judge visibly extends his arm and touches the standing woman's cheek** in the sampled images. The Qwen machine output erroneously assigned that reaching/touching action to **the standing woman**. That is a material **subject/patient reversal** capable of creating a false stage direction. The source-derived stills visually show a hand reaching across, but do not independently certify the entire moving shot, sound, physical-comedy intent or actor identities. The machine output also mixes camera/staging with inferred emotion or dignity unsupported by those stills. **Keep every field deferred.**

In S09E19's 555.8–557.8s ten-frame sequence, two men appear at a courtroom table, and the suited man handles a small dark object while the seated figure gestures. The original Qwen response came back **without a complete parseable ten-field JSON object**; the preserved 800-character failure excerpt includes speculative object-identity wording, but incomplete model prose must **not** be interpreted as a validated visual event. The fixed 256 output-token budget **might** have limited generation, but the partial report and parser result alone cannot prove the exact cause. No screenshot absence is proof a different action never happened between sampled frames.

**Quality conclusion:** real Qwen headless original-footage processing works, and **per-field stage truth does not follow from valid JSON**. On this action-rich pair, 1/2 reports passed strict JSON, and the valid report made the central observed actor-role mistake. Do not promote the model to an unattended screenplay narrator or extrapolate this tiny sample to 193 episodes. Source original dialogue witnesses remain unchanged and 0/193 complete episodes independently AV-certified.

## Next highest-value engineering and editorial moves

1. **Agent/patient review** for `actions`, `physical_comedy`, `reactions` and `proposed_stage_direction`: review who initiated what action and who/what received it, using the actual continuous original source video, never model prose alone. Prioritize this above speculative mood/adjectives.
2. **Missing/invalid model JSON must remain useful without becoming accepted script:** preserve exact model bytes and source SHA, original excerpt and explicit parse-error code. Queue all ten review fields as deferred; do not infer missing fields from the truncated response.
3. **One bounded temporal-sampling experiment:** compare original 1fps versus 2–3fps on exactly the same original clip, retaining source SHA and reviewing whether the actor-role inversion disappears. More frames alone do not prove a better result; report additional model seconds per independently accepted *physical action*, not simply higher JSON success.
4. **Prompt budget investigation before bulk CPU work:** test whether concise field-by-field JSON instructions reduce output truncation while keeping the required ten fields. Do not silently relax the existing JSON contract or fabricate confidence values. If the editor must rewrite the scene anyway, manual physical-business review may still be faster than Qwen's roughly three minutes per two-second clip.
5. **Film truth approval still open:** until original movie **in motion** has been watched and stage decisions attested, sampled image support and negative control classifications cannot yield a production screenplay passage.

The complete private owner review package can contain copyrighted stills and unaccepted model prose, but **the Git repository retains numeric/category-only evidence**, source hashes, operational plans and code. This prevents accidental archival promotion of film visuals or unreviewed descriptions.
