## Non-negotiable epistemic and editorial rules

Transcribed source text is not the same as video-verified dialogue; CTC and Whisper timing agreement is not independent listening; a caption gap is not an actual camera scene; frame sampling is not continuous motion; anonymous voice embeddings are not identifiable characters; acoustic contours are not proven emotions; a script scaffold is not a finished screenplay. Preserve uncertainty, immutable source material, licensing and the original-series-only scope.

The user's broad autonomy includes research, scripts, tests, PRs, safe workflow triggering, and documentation; it does **not** authorize false acceptance, unreviewed publication or exposing media/credentials.
