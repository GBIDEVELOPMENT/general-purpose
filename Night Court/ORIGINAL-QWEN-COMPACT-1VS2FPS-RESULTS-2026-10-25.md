**Observed performance is surprising, not causal proof.** Doubling input frames happened to coincide with dramatically lower model inference wall time. Generated answer lengths, decoder behavior, backend CPU scheduler and runner environment are possible confounders; only two samples were obtained. We cannot claim FPS makes Qwen inherently faster, establish a p95, or predict throughput over 193 episodes.

**Persistent correctness failure:** S01E01 original-SHA-derived nominal 5fps stills show the seated judge extending a hand toward the standing woman's cheek. At 2fps Qwen still describes the *standing person initiating contact with the seated person*, and now names the arm as the touched body part. Both descriptions conflict with the sampled source-derived reference. Increasing cadence alone did **not** fix actor/recipient role assignment in this controlled clip. S09E19 produces valid structured text about a document and pen/object interaction but the exact object identification, hand ownership and interpretation of any conversation remain unverifiable from those stills. The source-derived frames are not a full original moving audiovisual review, so **no formal model event precision/recall or screenplay action acceptance is claimed**.

This establishes a useful decision point: do **not** reflexively raise FPS again or infer bulk narration readiness from valid JSON. For physical-business notes, independent review of the *continuous original film* should identify who moved, what was touched/handed, the recipient and objective action timing. An annotation-first process is likely more valuable than additional expensive Qwen CPU prompts until we have a proper event-labeled evaluator. A human-generated stage note can then be source-linked to the held transcript without rewriting original subtitle dialogue. All model descriptions remain deferred, and **0/193 complete episodes** independently AV certified.

The owner-only inert source trigger [#422](https://github.com/gamesbyian/general-purpose/pull/422) must be closed unmerged after this evidence is recorded. Future source-video trials require a fresh, deliberately scoped owner authorization and measurable independent editorial benefit.
