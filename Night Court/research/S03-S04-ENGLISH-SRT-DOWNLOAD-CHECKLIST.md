## Upload and provenance

Keep each SRT's original filename and ideally the source URL in an adjacent per-file `.meta.json`. If bundling, produce `Night-Court-S03-subtitles.zip` and `Night-Court-S04-subtitles.zip` with one SRT per episode and an accompanying per-file source manifest. Preserve the 1984 series identity; don't mix the revival. Avoid renaming original source files before hashing. Open a PR or provide the branch link after upload. No passwords, DRM bypasses, or site access controls are required.
