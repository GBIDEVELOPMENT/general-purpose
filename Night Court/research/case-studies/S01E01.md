# S01E01: All You Need Is Love (first-pass fact sheet)

**Original broadcast:** 1984-01-04. **Director:** James Burrows. **Writer:** Reinhold Weege. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Night_Court_episodes

**Fictional premise:** Harry Stone begins his judgeship. The night staff initially mistrust his informal courtroom approach, while a domestic-dispute case tests whether his unconventional efforts can substitute for ordinary courtroom formality. Sources: https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/1489/night-court/episodeguide and https://transcripts.foreverdreaming.org/viewtopic.php?t=82333

**Cast context:** The pilot includes Gail Strickland as public defender Sheila Gardner; subsequent regular public defenders change. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Night_Court_episodes

**Analytical hypotheses for later scene-level verification:** Harry's playful demeanor juxtaposed with consequential legal decisions; authoritarian courtroom ritual inverted through magic-show and carnival-like interruptions; skeptical colleagues serve as the audience's straight characters. These are proposed interpretive tags, not scene-complete annotations.
