## Policy and boundaries
- User-owned FTP tree `/downloads`. **Movies and television remain mixed** at the root;
  series, season and film-franchise folders are fine.
- User explicitly approved deleting NFO release metadata, screenshot images and
  Screens directories, and multipart RAR archives positively matched to extracted videos.
- Never delete or relabel a questionable media file, local artwork, unextracted archive
  or cross-season multi-episode file based on guessed metadata.
- Media renames use exact source path, size, modification time, unique destination,
  complete-batch FTP source preflight, and per-file journal. Retain all audit artifacts.
- Warn that changing files can break seeding jobs referring to original paths.
